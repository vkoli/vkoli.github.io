Similar Ratings, Different Consensus: Visualizing Agreement Uncertainty in Survey Data​

Vrushali Koli, Yi Meng, Eugene P. Deess, and Aritra Dasgupta

Workshop on Uncertainty Visualization (UncertaintyVis), 2026 — to appear

Paper

Survey results are often summarized with a single agreement score, but on bounded rating scales the same score can come from very different response distributions. We call this ambiguity agreement uncertainty and introduce residual consensus, a calibration that expresses agreement relative to what the scale allows, shown in a coordinated view alongside the response distribution. We demonstrate it on real-world course-evaluation survey data.

Sensemaking of Socially-Mediated Crisis Information​

Vrushali Koli, Jun Yuan, and Aritra Dasgupta

Third Workshop on Bridging Human–Computer Interaction and Natural Language Processing (HCI+NLP), ACL 2024

Paper DOI

During a crisis, people turn to social media for answers but must separate credible, actionable information from noise. Drawing on crisis communication literature, we propose an AI-driven sensemaking model that contrasts expert guidance with subjective opinion and interprets messages based on the author's communicative intent, illustrated with examples from a COVID-19 tweet collection and a hypothetical usage scenario.
